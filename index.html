The Trust Patterns III / VIII

Designing AI people actually trust.

The hard part of AI design isn't the model — it's making people trust it. These are three of the repeatable patterns we use to do that. More in the work.

Pattern 01

Intent Preview

Show people what the AI is about to do before it does it. Trust is granted with eyes open — not recovered after a surprise.

Pattern 02

Confidence Halo

Make the model’s certainty visible. When people can see how sure the system is, they know when to lean in and when to look twice.

Pattern 03

Recovery Lane

Design the way back. When the AI gets it wrong — and it will — the path to correct, undo, or escalate should be obvious and unembarrassing.

About VII / VIII
Plate VII Ananya Kamra · Founder

I'm Ananya Kamra — a product designer and strategist with a background in enterprise systems, AI products, and complex digital experiences. After years of designing tools people relied on every day, one pattern kept repeating: teams would build something technically impressive, then quietly wonder why trust, adoption, and connection never followed. I started Cenra to work closer to that missing layer — where design shapes how a product is perceived, how clearly it speaks, and how intelligence feels to live with. The work spans products and brands; the question underneath is always the same — how does this earn confidence? And lately, a second question sits beside it: as AI makes competent design free, most brands are drifting toward the same quiet, lifeless minimalism — and I care about the opposite, about helping a brand keep the specific, characterful, alive thing that makes it itself. I work with a small number of founders and teams at a time, on purpose.
